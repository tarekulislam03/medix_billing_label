Build a React web app called "Medix Billing" for a medicine home-delivery business in India. The owner enters an order manually, the app calculates everything automatically, and it prints two documents: a customer receipt and a delivery label with a QR code.

TECH STACK
- React + Vite, plain CSS or Tailwind, no backend.
- Save orders and settings in localStorage.
- qrcode.react for QR codes, html5-qrcode for scanning.
- Printing via window.print() with print-only CSS (and a "Download PDF" option using html2pdf.js or jsPDF).
- Mobile-friendly. Currency is ₹ with Indian number format (en-IN).

SETTINGS (all editable on a Settings page, stored in localStorage; defaults below)
- Business name: Medix. Phone, address and a short tagline are editable.
- Minimum order: 500
- Tier 1 discount (order MRP total under 1500): 15%
- Tier 2 discount (order MRP total 1500 or above): 17%
- Tier threshold: 1500
- Customer fee (delivery + platform combined): 15
- Pharmacy discount to me: 15% of MRP
- Pharmacy commission to me: 3% of MRP
- My delivery boy cost per order: 25

CREATE ORDER PAGE
1. Customer details (manual): name, phone (10-digit validation), full address, optional notes.
2. Medicine rows (manual, add/remove rows): medicine name, MRP per unit, quantity, and an auto-calculated line total (MRP x qty). Add an optional "Not available" checkbox per row that excludes it from the bill.
3. Payment mode: COD or Paid (UPI).
4. Live calculation panel that updates as I type:
   - Total MRP = sum of (MRP x qty) for available items
   - Discount % = tier 1 or tier 2 based on Total MRP vs threshold (applied automatically, shown as a label like "17% tier applied")
   - Discount amount = Total MRP x discount %
   - Fee = customer fee (15)
   - Amount to pay = Total MRP - discount + fee
   - You save = discount amount
   - Show a warning if Total MRP is below the minimum order, and a hint like "Add ₹X more to unlock 17% off" when it is within ₹300 of the threshold.
5. Owner-only profit panel (collapsible, NEVER printed on any receipt):
   - Customer pays for medicines = Total MRP - discount
   - Pharmacy bill = Total MRP x (1 - pharmacy discount %)
   - Commission = Total MRP x commission %
   - Profit = (customer medicine payment - pharmacy bill) + commission + fee - delivery cost
   - Show profit in green if >= 6, amber if 0 to 5, red if negative.
6. Buttons: "Save order", "Print customer receipt", "Print delivery label", "Save and print both".

ECONOMICS RULES (must match exactly)
- Margin I get on MRP is 18% (15% pharmacy discount + 3% commission).
- Under 1500: customer gets 15%, so I keep 3%. At 1500 or above: customer gets 17%, so I keep 1%.
- Profit formula: (18% - discount%) x Total MRP + fee - delivery cost.
- Examples to unit test:
  - MRP 1000: discount 150, pays 865, profit 20
  - MRP 1500: discount 255, pays 1260, profit 5
  - MRP 2000: discount 340, pays 1675, profit 10
- Round money to 2 decimals internally and display rounded to the nearest rupee on receipts (no stray paise).

DOCUMENT 1: CUSTOMER RECEIPT (A5 or A4, clean, brand green #14532d with lime accent)the customer
- Header: Medix logo text, phone, address
- Order number (format MDX-YYYYMMDD-001, auto-increment per day), date and time
- Customer name, phone, address
- Items table: #, medicine, qty, MRP, line total (unavailable items shown struck out as "Not available")
- Summary: Total MRP, Discount (17%), Delivery + platform fee, Amount to pay, You saved, payment mode
- Footer: "Delivery within 24 hours", thank you line, and a small English + Bengali line (e.g. "ধন্যবাদ! মেডিক্সের সাথে থাকার জন্য।")
- Do NOT show profit, pharmacy cost or commission anywhere on it.

DOCUMENT 2: DELIVERY LABEL (small, 4x6 inch / 100x150 mm, thermal-printer friendly, black and white)
- Order number in large text
- Customer name, phone and full address in large readable text
- Amount to collect (big and bold): "COLLECT ₹1,260 (COD)" or "PAID - DO NOT COLLECT"
- Number of items and notes
- QR code encoding JSON: {"order":"MDX-...","phone":"...","amt":...,"mode":"COD"}
- Date and a "Handle with care - Medicines" line
- No pricing breakdown, no profit info.

SCAN AND DELIVER PAGE
- Camera QR scanner page. Scanning a label opens that order, shows customer details and the amount to collect, and has a "Mark as delivered" button that stores delivered time.
- Order statuses: Pending, Out for delivery, Delivered, Cancelled.

ORDERS PAGE
- Table of all orders with search (name, phone, order no), date filter and status filter.
- Click an order to view, edit, reprint either document, or change status.
- Summary cards for today and for the selected range: total orders, total sales, total profit, average profit per order. Add a "Export CSV" button.

EXTRA
- Validate that MRP and qty are positive numbers; qty must be an integer.
- Keyboard-friendly: Enter on the last field adds a new medicine row.
- Duplicate order button (reuse the customer and items).
- A reset/backup option: export and import all data as JSON.
- Clean, minimal UI in green tones, large touch targets, works well on a phone.

Deliver the full project structure, all code, install and run instructions, and a short README. Keep the pricing logic in one pure function (calculateBill(items, settings)) with unit tests for the three examples above.
